Evaluation

One metric. Organizer-verified results.

The official leaderboard measures the fraction of trials that complete both pickup and placement.

Success rate (%) = 100 × successful trials ÷ total trials

Higher is better. For example, 18 successful trials out of 20 produce a score of 90%.

Evaluation flow

What happens after you submit

  1. Organizers review your model links, pinned revisions, inference instructions, and access requirements.
  2. The referenced model and code are downloaded for evaluation on organizer infrastructure.
  3. Your policy runs under the common pick-and-place protocol. Organizers record successful and total trials.
  4. The verified percentage is published to the official leaderboard after evaluation.

Submissions remain awaiting evaluation until this process completes. Your upload does not start an automatic robot run.

Protocol

Consistent conditions for every entry

The robot or simulator, initial conditions, target-placement tolerance, time limit, trial count, and protocol version are being finalized. Organizers will publish these details before official evaluations begin.

A complete pickup and placement is required for a successful trial. Development screenshots and demonstration results are not a substitute for the official protocol.

Self-reported results

Share your experiments

You may report your own success rate and successful/total trial counts in the optional submission fields. Describe the robot or simulator and success criteria used.

Self-reported scores are unverified metadata and do not determine the official ranking.
