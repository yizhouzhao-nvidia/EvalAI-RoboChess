Evaluation

The official ranking uses only organizer-verified basic pick-and-place success rate:

Success rate (%) = 100 × successful trials / total trials. Higher is better. A successful trial must complete both pickup and placement.

Organizers will publish the robot or simulator, initial conditions, placement tolerances, time limit, trial count, and protocol version before official evaluation. These details are being finalized.

Evaluation is performed locally by organizers. Submissions remain awaiting evaluation until results are published.

Participants may optionally report their success rate, trial counts, and protocol as metadata. These claims are unverified and never determine the official ranking.
