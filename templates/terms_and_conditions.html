Participation

Submission terms

Artifact access and licenses

Provide references only to models, code, and assets you are authorized to make available for organizer evaluation. Include applicable licenses and keep the exact submitted revisions accessible throughout evaluation.

Arrange organizer access to private or gated repositories separately. Do not include credentials in the ZIP.

Evaluation and reporting

Organizers download and run submitted policies locally. The official ranking uses organizer-verified pick-and-place success rate. Self-reported results must describe their protocol and are not official scores.

Private submissions remain accessible to organizers. Public leaderboard participation follows your submission visibility settings.

Schedule and final protocol

The model submission window runs October 14–November 7, 2026, Pacific time. Final evaluation conditions and any additional participation rules will be published before official evaluations begin.
