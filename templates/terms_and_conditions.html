Submission terms

Provide references only to checkpoints, code, and assets you are authorized to make available for organizer evaluation. Include applicable licenses and document dependencies.

Keep pinned model and code revisions accessible throughout evaluation. Arrange access to private or gated repositories before evaluation; do not include credentials in your ZIP.

Organizers download and run policies for local robotic evaluation. Participant-reported scores must include their evaluation protocol.

Official ranking uses organizer-verified results. Private submissions remain accessible to organizers.

Final participation rules and evaluation protocol will be published before launch.
