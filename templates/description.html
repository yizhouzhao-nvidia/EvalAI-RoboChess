The benchmark

Pick. Move. Place.

Chess pieces provide a compact manipulation task: grasp a piece, lift it clear of the board, and place it at the designated target.

One official metric

Pick-and-place success rate (%). Every successful trial must complete both pickup and placement.

Your model, hosted elsewhere

Keep weights on Hugging Face or another accessible host. Submit a small ZIP of pinned links and inference instructions.

Verified by organizers

Organizers fetch your policy and evaluate it locally. Optional self-reported scores are recorded separately.

The broader RoboChess project explores board perception, chess moves, and simulation-to-real transfer. This EvalAI benchmark ranks basic manipulation success; chess-game performance is outside this ranking.

How to participate

From model link to verified score

01 · Prepare

Host your weights and pin the model revision. Pin your inference-code commit and describe the observation and action interfaces.

02 · Submit

Upload an instructions ZIP in EvalAI’s submission tab. Include setup and inference commands, runtime requirements, and artifact access details.

03 · Evaluate

Organizers download your references, run local trials, and publish verified success rates. Uploading does not generate an immediate score.
